Background removal - done for cells, with a limit

Local Pillow and numpy, luminance alpha: 0 below 18, ramp to 255 at 55. Dark blue and purple interiors can become semi-transparent, because they are close to black. For print, the dark JPG on a black tee or mug is safer than the transparent PNG. Transparent PNGs are a bonus, not print-ready finals. A human check per cell is still needed before any live print.
